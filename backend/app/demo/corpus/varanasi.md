# Overview
Varanasi, also called Banaras or Kashi, lies on the western bank of the Ganga in Uttar Pradesh. It is one of the world's oldest continuously inhabited cities and one of the holiest cities in Hinduism. The riverfront is lined with more than eighty ghats — stone steps leading down to the water.

# Ghats and the Ganga Aarti
Dashashwamedh Ghat is the busiest ghat and hosts the elaborate Ganga Aarti every evening around sunset. Arrive early for a good spot, or watch from a boat. Assi Ghat, at the southern end, holds a morning programme of aarti, music and yoga.

A sunrise boat ride along the ghats is one of the most memorable experiences in Varanasi. Agree on the price and duration with the boatman before boarding.

Manikarnika Ghat is the main cremation ghat. Visitors should be respectful; photographing cremations is considered deeply disrespectful and is not allowed.

# Temples
Kashi Vishwanath Temple, dedicated to Shiva, is one of the twelve jyotirlingas. The Kashi Vishwanath Corridor, linking the temple to the river, was inaugurated in December 2021. Mobile phones, cameras and bags are generally not allowed inside; lockers are available nearby. Expect security checks and queues.

# Sarnath
Sarnath, about 10 km from the city centre, is where the Buddha is believed to have given his first sermon. It has the Dhamek Stupa, temple ruins and the Sarnath Museum, which displays the Lion Capital of Ashoka — the source of India's national emblem. Check the museum's weekly closing day before you go.

# Food and shopping
Try kachori-sabzi for breakfast, lassi in clay cups, Banarasi paan, and malaiyo, a frothy milk sweet available in winter. Banarasi silk saris are famous; buy from established shops or cooperatives.

# Practical tips
October to March is the most pleasant time to visit Varanasi; summers are very hot, and during the monsoon high water can submerge lower ghats and suspend boat rides. The old city is a maze of narrow lanes, so allow extra time and walk where possible. Two to three days covers the main experiences.
