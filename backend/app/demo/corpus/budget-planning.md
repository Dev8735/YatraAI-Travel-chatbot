> Demo knowledge-base article describing the planning assumptions YatraAI uses for rough budget estimates. These are not quotes or live prices. Actual costs vary widely by city, season, booking time and personal choices.

# How YatraAI estimates trip budgets
When no live pricing provider is connected, YatraAI gives only a rough planning range. It multiplies a per-person, per-day range by the number of days and travellers, and labels the result as an estimate. Intercity transport (flights and long-distance trains) is not included, because prices change constantly.

# Per-person, per-day planning ranges (INR)
- Budget travel: about ₹1,500 to ₹3,000 per person per day — hostels, dormitories or simple guesthouses, local food, public transport and mostly low-cost sights.
- Mid-range travel: about ₹3,000 to ₹7,000 per person per day — comfortable hotels, a mix of restaurants, some taxis and paid attractions.
- Luxury travel: about ₹10,000 to ₹25,000 or more per person per day — upscale or heritage hotels, private cars and guided tours.

# What changes trip costs the most
Peak season (for example, winter holidays in Goa or Rajasthan, and long weekends) can push hotel prices much higher. Hill stations and islands often cost more because of transport. Booking early usually helps. Entry tickets at major monuments are often higher for foreign nationals than for Indian citizens.

# Saving tips
Travel in shoulder seasons, use trains for long distances, eat at busy local restaurants, and group nearby sights on the same day to cut local transport costs.
