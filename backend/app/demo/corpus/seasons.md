# Winter (roughly October to March)
Winter is the most comfortable season for most of the plains and for Rajasthan, Gujarat, Goa, Kerala's coast and much of central and southern India. December and January can be cold in north India, with fog that may delay flights and trains. Higher Himalayan areas get snow, and some high mountain roads close.

# Summer (roughly April to June)
The plains of north and central India become very hot in summer. This is the main season for Himalayan hill stations such as Shimla and Manali. High-altitude Ladakh usually opens to most road travel from around May or June.

# Monsoon (roughly June to September)
The south-west monsoon usually reaches Kerala in early June and spreads across most of India by July. It brings lush landscapes and lower prices, but also landslides in the hills, flooding in some areas and rough seas on the west coast. Some national parks close to visitors for the monsoon.

# Post-monsoon (roughly September to November)
Landscapes are green and the weather starts to cool after the monsoon. This period is popular for festivals such as Navratri, Durga Puja and Diwali, which also make travel busier and more expensive.

# Choosing a destination by season
- Heritage cities of Rajasthan and Gujarat: October to March.
- Goa beaches: November to February for dry weather.
- Kerala: September to March; the monsoon suits Ayurveda retreats.
- Himalayan hill stations: April to June and September to November.
