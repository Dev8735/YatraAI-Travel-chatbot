# Overview
Rishikesh is a town in Uttarakhand in the foothills of the Himalayas, where the Ganga leaves the mountains. It is known as a centre for yoga and meditation and is often called the "Yoga Capital of the World". An International Yoga Festival is held in Rishikesh every year, usually in March.

# Spiritual sights
The evening Ganga Aarti at Parmarth Niketan and at Triveni Ghat draws large crowds; arrive early. The Beatles Ashram (Chaurasi Kutia), where the Beatles stayed in 1968, is now a ruin decorated with murals and lies within the Rajaji Tiger Reserve area; an entry ticket is charged.

Laxman Jhula, the town's famous suspension bridge, was closed in 2019 because of structural safety concerns, and a replacement bridge has been under construction nearby. Ram Jhula is the other main suspension bridge. Check locally which crossings are currently open.

# Adventure
Rishikesh is a popular base for white-water rafting on the Ganga. Common stretches start upstream at places such as Shivpuri and Kaudiyala. Rafting generally runs from around September or October to June and is suspended during the peak monsoon months for safety. Choose a licensed operator and always wear the life jacket and helmet provided.

Short hikes lead to Neer Garh Waterfall, a few kilometres from Laxman Jhula.

# Local rules
Rishikesh is a pilgrimage town: meat and alcohol are not served in the main town area. Dress modestly at ashrams and temples.

# Getting there
Haridwar is about 25 km from Rishikesh and has more train connections. The nearest airport is Jolly Grant Airport near Dehradun. Two to three days is enough for a relaxed visit; add more if you plan a yoga course.
